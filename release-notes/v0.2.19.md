Alpha 0.2.19 makes conversations and their real worker terminals easier to reach.

- Horizontal conversation tabs have a fixed-right New conversation button, keyboard navigation and per-conversation Alpha/terminal view choices.
- Right-click a tab or the conversation background for Search, details, Close conversation and History. Closing archives only the intended conversation; its draft, history, workers and pending Questions remain available. History remains reachable after closing the last conversation.
- Workspace stays visible at wide and narrow widths. The old Return to conversation control is removed; resizing and the bottom message composer remain available.
- Real terminal input is explicitly unlocked and routed to the chosen worker in that conversation. Switching views does not launch, interrupt or approve worker activity.
- Attention badges and on-demand important events replace the permanent notification area. Questions stay in Questions, routine progress in the status bar, and event links navigate to their own origin.
- Automated Codex delivery now waits for a recognized empty composer, submits idle requests, queues busy follow-ups without interruption and verifies exact native input records. Receipts distinguish paste, submission/queue and acceptance; uncertain delivery is never replayed automatically.
- A fixed blue square with a filled white brain identifies Alpha across themes. Theme artwork, voice personas and optional 3D presence are retained.

Verification includes isolated Windows PTYs, close/reopen persistence, keyboard and narrow-layout checks, theme-invariant icon rendering, and real isolated Codex idle/busy delivery without manual submit keys. The release workflow checks service/UI tests, Rust, MSI structure and packaged runtime.

Known boundaries: real Claude submission and owner-installed native interaction need separate review. Busy Claude automation is withheld when safe queue semantics are not verified. Unknown prompts and existing terminal drafts require direct inspection. A worker turn finishing is not proof that its task is verified complete.

Windows x64 MSI; currently unsigned. The accompanying alpha-update.json records the exact installer size and SHA-256. Publication is separate from installation; use Alpha's supported updater and retain session restart notes before restarting.
